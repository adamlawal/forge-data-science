Capstone reflection prompts
About 400 to 700 words, written in the first person.

Please write a continuous reflection, not a list of one-line answers. These
prompts are a guide for what to cover.

1. What question did you set out to answer? Did the question change, and why?

2. Describe one decision you made (a cleaning choice, a metric, a comparison,
   a decision not to use a model, or a decision to keep one). Why did you
   make it that way?

3. What was harder than you expected? How did you handle it?

4. What would you do differently if you had one more week?

5. What can you now do, in concrete terms, that you could not do at the
   start of the course? Give a real example from this project if you can.

Please do not paste the README, and please do not write a list of tools.
