7 to 10 minute presentation outline
Forge Academy capstone

Use this as a skeleton. You can move the minute marks, but keep the jobs.

Minute 0 to 1. The question
    One or two sentences. Why would someone care about the answer?

Minute 1 to 3. The data and what you prepared
    Grain, size, time window, and one cleaning or scope choice that mattered.
    Do not narrate every step.

Minute 3 to 7. Findings
    One or two findings, each with a number and a comparison.
    One chart or table is enough if the title states the takeaway.
    If you used a model, say whether it beat the baseline and what that means.
    Do not tour every cell.

Minute 7 to 9. Recommendation, limitation, next step
    What you would do, or not do.
    What the work cannot claim.
    What you would do with one more week.

Minute 9 to 10. Close
    The one number you want us to remember, in one sentence.

Timer habit
    Practise once out loud with a clock. If you overrun in rehearsal, cut a demo,
    not the limitation.
